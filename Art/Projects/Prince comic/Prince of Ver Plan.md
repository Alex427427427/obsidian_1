### Sequence order

1. **Dream:** Maya meets the Prince. “Do you wish that your life was free and beautiful?” She wakes crying, unsure why.
2. **Town:** Establish her year of caring for her crippled, depressed sister after their parents' death. Sister disappeared a month ago; cause unrevealed. Maya dresses up, flirts with the visiting researcher, then sees the Prince on the bridge.
3. **Dream:** Maya properly meets the Prince. They dance. He begins showing her impossible worlds.
4. **Courtship:** Over several dreams, the Prince shows Maya cosmic art and talks about Ver. Maya mentions her sister abandoned art after the accident.
5. **Investigation:** The researcher's wife disappears. He interrogates Maya and takes her to the beach, where **he sees the Black Orchard but Maya sees nothing.** She only sees the Prince.
6. **Evacuation:** The town empties. Maya finds the researcher's note saying he will join his wife.
7. **Final dream:** The Prince explains that Ver creates art from worlds it encounters. He says he has found something extraordinary on Earth. Maya thinks he means her; he means **Earth**. He says she is somehow connected to it.
8. **Ocean:** Maya encounters the Prince standing offshore with the researcher and his wife, who are beginning their journey toward Ver. She sees another body beneath the water.
9. **Sister:** Maya recognizes her sister as a beautiful marble post-human. Fragments reveal the diary, her sister reading it, Maya apologizing, and the suicide. Maya's grief becomes almost animalistic.
10. **Confrontation:** The Prince explains his purpose and invites Maya to Ver. Her sister tells Maya she must take responsibility for her death. Maya finally rejects the guilt:

> “You can't expect me to take relentless abuse and not wish to be free.”  
> “You ran into the ocean. You gave up on your life. You did this to yourself.”

Her sister cries marble tears.

11. **Departure:** Maya says:

> “I'm sorry I can't go with you.”

The Prince examines Maya's thread and discovers it is intertwined with Earth's.

> “Oh.”

He and her sister leave for Ver.

12. **Ending:** Maya watches them disappear.

> “Sorry I couldn't come with you.”

**Maya blinks.**

**Cut to empty ocean.**
