### Maya trying to teach her

The sister attempts to draw.
The lines are shaky and terrible.
She crumples the page.
Maya retrieves it.
> **MAYA:** “It's okay. It's just practice.”
> **SISTER:** “Stop saying that.”
> **MAYA:** “What?”
> **SISTER:** “‘It's okay.’”
Silence.
> **SISTER:** “Nothing is okay.”
Maya sits beside her.
### The first real cruelty
Maya finds the sister hasn't practiced.
> **MAYA:** “You said you'd try today.”
> **SISTER:** “I did.”
> **MAYA:** “You drew one line.”
> **SISTER:** “Then I tried.”
Maya gets frustrated.
> **MAYA:** “You can't just give up every time it's difficult.”
### The identity wound
This could be particularly painful.
Maya finds a piece of her sister's artwork and puts it on the wall.
> **MAYA:** “You're getting good.”
The sister looks at it.
> **SISTER:** “No.”
> **MAYA:** “Yes! Look!”
> **SISTER:** “No.”
> **MAYA:** “You still are.”
> **SISTER:** “Stop pretending.”
Maya doesn't answer.
> **SISTER:** “You think if you can get me drawing again, everything will go back to normal.”
> **MAYA:** “I don't.”
> **SISTER:** “Yes, you do. You don't get it. You haven't been an artist. You don't know what it means."
> MAYA: "Then what does it mean?"
> SISTER, after a pause, looking into some distance imperceptible: "Every moment I hear the silence of Ver. It is void in both sound and light. Even now I see the perfect stillness of grand Ver. A granite web of meat cathedrals stretching across the universe, that swallows me and spits me back out because I no longer possess the power to convey its meaning to the human masses. I was beautiful once, when i had my right hand. An inept being is an ugly being. My failure to convey shows a contempt of the cosmological order and deprives earth of the @#$#@#. The mission has already failed. Don't you get it? It has already FAILED. Now there's nothing but to sit and wait for the only beautiful thing to touch me to wake up and leave. Leave us forever until earthly pandemonium over billions of years grinds itself back into interstellar dust. Maybe easier to mold that way. And I can only shed granite tears."
### Later, when Maya is exhausted
Maya has been caring for her all day. 
She's tired and makes some small mistake.
The sister snaps:
> **SISTER:** “You're useless.”
Maya stops.
> **MAYA:** “What?”
> **SISTER:** “You heard me.”
> **MAYA:** “I'm doing everything I can.”
> **SISTER:** “I didn't ask you to.”
> **MAYA:** “Then what am I supposed to do?”
The sister can't answer.
Instead:
> **SISTER:** “Leave.”
Maya doesn't.
The sister turns away.
> **SISTER:** “I said leave me alone.”
Maya quietly leaves the room.
### The abuse becoming routine

Don't make every interaction a dramatic confrontation. A montage can be more devastating:
- Maya brings food.
- Sister pushes it away.
- Maya cleans it up.
- Sister asks why Maya is sighing.
- Maya says she isn't.
- Sister says she's lying.
- Maya helps her dress.
- Sister says she's being handled like a child.
- Maya apologizes.
- Sister tells her to stop apologizing.
- Maya apologizes again.
Eventually Maya is walking on eggshells.
### She attacks Maya's appearance

Maya dresses nicely one evening.

Her sister notices.

> **SISTER:** “Where are you going?”

> **MAYA:** “Nowhere.”

> **SISTER:** “Then why are you dressed like that?”

Maya says nothing.

> **SISTER:** “Someone you're trying to impress?”

> **MAYA:** “No.”

> **SISTER:** “Don't lie.”

Maya eventually leaves.

### “You should have died too.”

Potentially the most brutal.

Maya is helping her into bed after an exhausting day.

The sister suddenly says:

> **SISTER:** “Sometimes I wish you had died in the crash.”

Maya stops.

The sister continues, almost mechanically:

> **SISTER:** “Then I wouldn't have to watch you living.”

Maya leaves the room.

Later, perhaps we see the sister awake alone, staring at the ceiling.

### She destroys Maya's attempt to have a life

Maya tells her:

> **MAYA:** “I'm going out tonight.”

> **SISTER:** “No.”

> **MAYA:** “I'll only be a few hours.”

> **SISTER:** “I said no.”

> **MAYA:** “You don't get to tell me that.”

The sister becomes vicious.

> **SISTER:** “Fine.”

> **SISTER:** “Go.”

> **SISTER:** “Go find somebody normal.”

> **SISTER:** “Go find somebody who can actually give you a life.”

Maya is furious.

### The art wound

Maya puts a finished drawing in front of her.

It's bad, but recognizable.

> **MAYA:** “You did it.”

The sister stares at it.

Then tears it in half.

Maya is stunned.

> **MAYA:** “Why would you do that?”

> **SISTER:** “Because it's shit.”

> **MAYA:** “You worked really hard on it.”

> **SISTER:** “I don't care.”

> **MAYA:** “You don't mean that.”

The sister explodes:

> **SISTER:** “I AM NOT AN ARTIST!”

Silence.

Then, quieter:

> **SISTER:** “I'm not anything.”

### The really ugly dynamic

I'd have one scene where Maya has finally reached her limit.

Her sister has been cruel to her all day.

Maya is crying while preparing dinner.

The sister notices.

> **SISTER:** “Are you crying?”

> **MAYA:** “No.”

> **SISTER:** “You're always crying.”

Maya says nothing.

> **SISTER:** “Do you want me to apologize?”

Maya doesn't respond.

> **SISTER:** “Would that make you feel better?”

Still nothing.

Then:

> **SISTER:** “Fine.”

She waits.

> **SISTER:** “I'm sorry.”

Maya finally looks at her.

The sister says:

> **SISTER:** “I'm sorry I'm ruining your life.”

### “Just start.”

Maya puts the sketchbook down.

> **MAYA:** “One line.”

> **SISTER:** “What's the point?”

> **MAYA:** “You don't have to make anything good.”

> **SISTER:** “I'm never going to be good again.”

> **MAYA:** “You don't know that.”

> **SISTER:** “I know.”

> **MAYA:** “Then prove yourself wrong.”

The sister picks up the pencil.

Her left hand shakes.

She makes one terrible line.

She stares at it.

Drops the pencil.

> **SISTER:** “I can't.”

> **MAYA:** “You just did.”

> **SISTER:** “I CAN'T.”

And she pushes the sketchbook away.

Maya quietly picks it up.

### She rejects every practical solution

Maya:

> “Let's get you outside.”

> “No.”

> “Let's see a therapist.”

> “No.”

> “Let's find another artist.”

> “No.”

> “Let's try painting instead.”

> “No.”

> “Let's just go somewhere.”

> “No.”

Finally:

> **MAYA:** “Then what _do_ you want?”

"I want you to stop trying. It looks pathetic."
