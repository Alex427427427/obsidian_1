
Prince of Ver

### Act 1 
A woman sleeps. 

Narration: I first saw the prince in a dream. I was under a black empty sky. Around me was a barren world of white sand... and an ocean of stars. Despite the air being completely still, waves moved in from far away. All the trillions of stars hugged the shore, as if afraid of what was coming from the distant ocean. I squinted at the horizon and saw him.

Full page view of the dark horizon, and a small white humanoid form in the distance. Title drop Prince of Ver on the same page. 

The woman blinks, and the prince's torso now fills up her view - the prince has moved right up to her. This startles her. 

Narration: Seeing his face, something primal stirred in my stomach. His eyes held the blackness of space. He was... impossibly... beau-

Prince, with pauses: "Do you - wish that your life - was free and beautiful - Maya of mankind?"

The prince's eyes widen to impossible proportions for a split second, as if a predator aroused by the identification of prey. 

Jump cut to Maya waking up. Her bedroom is messy. 

Narration: I woke up crying, not remembering why. Morning had arrived. Another day in Grey Point. I hated this town. 

Montage of the things she hates.

Narration: The faint smell of rotting seaweed... the same tired people and their vacant faces... and the feeling that nothing here ever changed. 

Cut to her driving. 

Narration: The road design of Grey Point was especially horrific. It was impossible to know if a car was around the corner... Yet idiots rushed up and down these bends like it was a race track. They'd estimated that fixing all these bends would've cost $3M. This was a small town; there was no need to spend $3M on it. I suppose that my parents' lives were not worth $3M to the residents of Grey Point. 

A car appears behind the bend, almost running into Maya's car. They beep. Maya spits out her tongue with contempt. 

Narration: Despite all the ugliness... There was at least one thing here that i always considered beautiful... 

Cut to full page of Maya leaning on her sister's chair, watching her paint. The painting is of a woman in black robes, descending into some alien ocean. Black Cambrian pseudo plant lifeforms sway in the distance. 

Narration: My sister, Nova, and her art. How fortunate that she found the true love of her life so early. Unlike the town, the dark worlds she painted were always changing... never disappointing... at times unsettling... but always alluring... I remember thinking, even then, that I'd seen some of these places in my dreams. 

Narration: She posted her art online and slowly built a following. She'd grown up being praised for her talent, and she loved the attention. Eventually, she was able to make a living from commissions. Every year, there was an art convention in the city where I studied, and every year, Nova would be there. I would go too, without us ever arranging to meet, waiting for her to appear with her strange little worlds. For a while, she got to live doing what she loved. I was so happy for her. She shone like a bright star.

Narration: After the crash, Nova and I received $16K in total from the local council. A $3k survivor grant for each of us; a $5k funeral grant for each parent killed. We spent most of it on Nova's surgery. I paused my degree, moved back here, and found a job at the water treatment plant so I could look after her. Nova recovered well, but...

Cut to Nova leaning on a bean bag. Her desk looms above her. Her drawing chair on the other side of the panel, out of reach.

Narration: She had lost her right arm. 

Maya and Nova share a meal. 

Narration: I could tell something was missing in her eyes. 

Narration: Nova disappeared. Now, this was how I spent my days. Replaying the events of those years. I think... things were really hard for her. 

Maya drives back home.

Maya: I had no one to come home to.

### Act 3
Maya dreams. The prince tells her "everyone else has left. no one can bother us anymore. Come meet me at the beach. Tonight we depart for Ver."

Maya runs to the beach. She does not see the prince. A voice comes from nowhere. 

"You came." 

Something rises from the sand. A black marble humanoid figure. It has freckles on its face. It could only be -

"Look at me."

Maya is horrified.

"I'm back together."

Nova: "The ugliness was not in Grey Point. It was in you."

Maya: "What?"

Nova, smiling: "Don't you remember? It was you who killed me." 

Flash back to Nova abusing Maya. Maya writing in her diary "If Nova wasn't here, my life would be free and beautiful."

Maya breaks down. She starts howling, kneeling in front of the marble statue that Nova has become. 

The prince makes a move. 

Maya, weakly: "who are you? why are you doing this to me?" 

Prince: "I come from a far away kingdom. I was tasked by the King and Queen to spread the art of our civilisation. Of planets and worlds, I mold; I am a sculptor from Ver."

Prince: "In order to make art, I need material. When I first arrived, I found that your world's material was tightly bound with life. I could not pull it apart. I've been searching for a long time, a place where the thread breaks, so that I can pull the strands apart, so that I can remake the world."

Maya: "And what... kind of place... is Ver?"

Prince, pointing upwards, smiling fondly: "Ver is a cold, dark and quiet region of that ocean. It is in fact its largest region. It is untouched by starlight. It is home to our grand civilisation." 

Narration: So that was where I would go. With Nova by my side. When I decided that Maya ended and something else began. And earth shall be left a dark cathedral, of water and of stone and of meat, drifting through space, like all the other lifeless planets the Prince showed me in our beautiful dreams.  

Nova: "It's time to take responsibility for your actions, Maya." 

Maya remembers even crueler abuses. but she also remembers something else. The choices that she consistently made. To care for Nova, even when hurting. to quit her uni, and take on a job, even though she had to give up her life. And Nova, who consistently chose to decay. 

The prince moves his hand over a kneeling Maya's head. there is a thread on her head, that attaches her to the rest of the world. It is soft and weak. His pupils widen. He reaches for Maya. 

Maya, still looking down, the reader not being able to see her face: "Nova. What do you know about responsibility?"

The marble does not move. 

Maya: "I tried. I tried really, really hard. But you- you cannot expect me to take relentless abuse and not wish to be free!" 

Maya, standing up, she is crying and screaming now, as if giving everything she had to fight for her life: "YOU gave up on YOUR life. YOU chose to walk into the ocean. You did all this to YOURSELF!!" 

Maya pants. Nova sheds marble tears. 

The prince sees that Maya's strand is still bound tightly to the rest of earth. 

The Prince: "Oh." 

Maya, crying: "I'm sorry, I can't come with you." 

A quiet sequence of the statue receding backwards, with the prince, into the horizon. 

Maya stares at the empty ocean. "I'm sorry..."

Time passes.
