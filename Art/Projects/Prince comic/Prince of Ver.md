The prince appears to Maya in a dream and asks her: "do you wish for a free and beautiful life?"

Maya wakes and notices herself crying. She goes about her day in her town. Everyone looks like disturbing, unmoving statues. This place is dead. The same people... the same thoughts... the same words... over and over. Maya tries to flirt with someone at a bar. But they too are a moving statue. Maybe the town notices her judgement. For she can sense they stare at her funny, as if she did something wrong. 

Maya laments how she can't bear to see the world like this anymore. That's when the prince appears on a bridge. She goes up to him, and tries to speak to him. Prince: "you look burdened."
