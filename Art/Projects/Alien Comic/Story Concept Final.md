### Core of the story
When grief-stricken and faced with a beautiful offer of escape from reality, do you take it? 
Patricia chooses illusion. Lucas chooses truth. Marco teaches Lucas to see reality with wonder. Susan tries to escape and perishes. 

Life is filled with suffering, chaos, and disappointment. Patricia: so escape it. Lucas: no. The emptiness of reality is far richer than any illusion. Instead of chasing a dream, Lucas pursues truth. 

Lucas doesn't need an arc. he simply needs to move from uncertainty to conviction. 

Marco is a dreamer, but unlike Patricia, he dreams because reality is astonishing. Patricia dreams because reality is disappointing. 
### Events
### Episode 1: Abductions
Lucas solves a murder in Vyshkanov. Elena, the murderer, finally chooses to face reality. 
We learn that some organisation like the Illuminati once had activity here. 
Vyshkanov had a group of scientists that frequently went into the forest and brought back samples. The scientists were testing propulsion there. To reach the space stations in orbit. it was the same town where the space telescopes observed dark matter. 
### Episode 2: Sightings
Reports of aliens sighted at Eurasian Academy of Sciences. Lucas traces to Susan. Pretends to be audit. Lucas discovers history of the lab dismantled by the government. The lab was dismantled because they found out what dark matter is. the villain of this episode is a scientist who has a vendetta against the government. 
### Episode 3: Missing
Teacher episode. Miss Liam. Class excursion. Mr Williams part of Illuminati. reveals things about them. Why he doesn't want to associate anymore. 
### Episode 4: Lumina Junction
Lucas remembering Marco's death. The beginning of his grief. We finally learn of his true journey. He finds the Illuminati in this episode. Marco's star. Find the Star of Eurasia. 

### Episode 5: Star Watcher
ARSE discover missing star. It is the abandoned space station Satellite 6. This is the Star of Eurasia. What is contained on it... are the constitution of the earth federation. 
Susan and Lucas celebrate, but Susan has the disease. They are on a race against the clock. The truth hides in the city darkness. 
### Episode 6: Disease
Susan and Lucas develop study of the disease spreading through Eurasia. 
Susan wants to leave Eurasia. get stopped by illuminati. get caught by eurasian government. Patricia takes them away but kills Susan.
